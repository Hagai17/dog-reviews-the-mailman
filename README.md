One funny one-line review that a dog gives the mailman, entered in the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-the-dog-reviews-the-mailman-87608f85).

`main.py` writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it:

> Two stars. He feeds the door instead of me. The door does not even eat. I have watched it not eat for three years.

The drafts and the local duels that picked this line are in `candidates.md`.
